Raising deductibles and premiums may reduce employer costs on paper, but it doesn’t address the real drivers of healthcare spending. In many cases, cost-shifting leads employees to delay or avoid care, resulting in worse health outcomes, higher long-term costs, and lower productivity. Discover why value-based care offers a more effective, sustainable way to control healthcare expenses without placing a greater financial burden on employees.
